Stops & the “insurance” question

net of 10 bps

You framed the dip entry as stop-loss insurance. Half true: you do get in lower — but a tight mechanical stop backfires. This is a mean-reversion trade, so positions are meant to sag before they recover; a −5% stop is hit on a third of trades and cuts winners short.

Stop-loss variants

Same signal, different exit rule.

RuleAvg / tradeWinWorstHit
Give it room. The insurance is the better entry basis, not a tight stop. If you must set one, put it below the typical dip (well past −4.4%), or size the position for that drawdown instead.
