# V1.0.0.1 — Futures & configurable bar size

The first increment on top of the [[V1000]] building blocks. Planned as a small loose-ends release through [[Cycle 1]]; in practice it became the **futures + bar-size** release, delivered across [[Cycle 1]] and [[Cycle 2]]. Released **2026-10-31** (last merge: PR #10, `d6b95af`).

## What shipped
- **Indicators:** MACD, ATR, Stochastic, ADX, `rsi_ema_cross`, `trading_days_offset_gain`, fixed `ema_arb` (registry = 14).
- **Data quality:** trade-aware stock dedupe + watchlist rebuild.
- **Futures ("contracts-as-stocks"):** schema (`parent_stock_id`, `sec_type`, `expiry`, `multiplier`), source-agnostic `ohlcv_store`, IB contract + term-structure retrieval, front-month selection, own continuous series (`CONTFUT`, `ES.c1`), `futures` CLI group, `update_futures` workflow.
- **Configurable bar size:** `price_bars` keyed by `(stock_id, bar_size, timestamp)` + `price_hourly` back-compat view; `1 min`…`1 hour` retrieval; intraday timeframes in `Asset`; `--bar-size` on the CLI; per-size incremental refresh.
- **Schema:** `init.sql` reconciled to `price_bars`.
- **Tests:** 135 passing (from 70 at the start of [[Cycle 1]]).
